- **Lawrence A. Gordon**
  - **Position**
    EY Alumni Professor of Managerial Accounting and Information Assurance at the University of Maryland's Robert H. Smith School of Business; also an Affiliate Professor at the UM Institute for Advanced Computer Studies.
  - **Education**
    Ph.D. in Managerial Economics from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute.
  - **Research focus**
    Managerial accounting and cybersecurity economics, including economic aspects of information security, corporate performance measures, cost management systems, and capital investments.
  - **Publications**
    Author of about 100 articles in journals including The Accounting Review, ACM Transactions on Information and System Security, Communications of the ACM, Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis, Journal of Computer Security, MIS Quarterly, Accounting Organizations and Society, and the Journal of Accounting and Public Policy.
  - **Gordon-Loeb Model**
    Co-created with Martin P. Loeb — a mathematical economic model for deriving an organization's optimal cybersecurity investment level, featured in The Wall Street Journal and The Financial Times.
  - **Books**
    - a. Managerial Accounting: Concepts and Empirical Evidence
    - b. Managing Cybersecurity Resources: A Cost-Benefit Analysis
    - c. Improving Capital Budgeting: A Decision Support System Approach
  - **Editorial roles**
    Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of Accounting and Public Policy; serves on the editorial boards of several other journals.
  - **Impact**
    Over 6,400 citations on Google Scholar.

  - **Content**

  - **Location**

- **Computer security**
  - **Definition**
    Also known as cyber security or IT security.
  - **Scope**
    Protects computer systems from theft or damage to hardware, software, or information, and from disruption or misdirection of the services they provide.
- **Dancing pigs**
  - **Definition**
    A computer security term describing users' attitude toward security.
  - **Behavior**
    Users continue to pick an amusing graphic even after a security warning that it is potentially dangerous, prioritizing desired features over security.
  - **Usage**
    Generally used by tech experts and found in IT articles.
- **International Computer Security Association**

  - **Mission**
    Increase awareness of the need for computer security and provide education about security products and technologies.

- **Physical Security Professional**
  - **Definition**
    A board certification process for individuals involved in the physical security of organizations, offered by ASIS International.
  - **Term**
    Certification lasts for three years.
  - **Recertification**
    Requires completing 60 "Continuing Professional Education" credits.
- **Rainbow Series**
  - **Definition**
    A series of computer security standards and guidelines published by the United States government in the 1980s and 1990s, also known as the Rainbow Books.
  - **Publishers**
    Originally published by the U.S. Department of Defense Computer Security Center, then by the National Computer Security Center.
