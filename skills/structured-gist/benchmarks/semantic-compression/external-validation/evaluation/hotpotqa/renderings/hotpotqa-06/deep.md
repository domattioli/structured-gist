    Second full Korean album "Crush," with lead single "Come Back Home"; entered the Billboard 200 chart.

    Released the farewell song "Goodbye" in January 2017 for fans, after announcing their disbandment.

- **Suraiya**
  - **Identity**
    Suraiya Jamaal Sheikh (15 June 1929 – 31 January 2004), an Indian Muslim Hindi/Hindustani film actress and playback singer in Bollywood from the 1940s to the early 1960s.
  - **Reputation**
    The most popular singing star of the Indian sub-continent of her generation.
  - **Personal life**
    - a. remained unmarried throughout her life
    - b. her love affair with actor Dev Anand was aborted by her maternal grandmother
    - c. was the only child of her parents
  - **Talent**
    Had a natural talent for singing, acting, and dancing from childhood.
  - **Training**
    - a. basic music training from a Hindustani music teacher ('masterji'), arranged by her mother
    - b. first sang for a children's program on All India Radio in 1937
    - c. later received on-the-job vocal training from Naushad while recording
    - d. dance training from Mumtaz Ali, dancer-actor and father of comedian Mehmood
  - **Early career (through 1943)**
    As a child singer, did playback singing for actress Mehtab in three films, and also sang in some of her own films for her roles as a child artiste.
  - **Acting career (1946–1963)**
    Sang only for her own films thereafter, and acted as a heroine from 1946 until the end of her career in 1963.
  - **Peak stardom (1948–1951)**
    Became Bollywood's singing superstar, earning more for her performances than all performing actors of the time, male or female, including Dev Anand, Raj Kapoor, Dilip Kumar, Nargis, and Madhubala.
  - **Earnings**
    - a. received 1.5–2 lakh rupees per film role
    - b. top male actors were getting 50,000 rupees to 1 lakh
    - c. commanded Rs. 1,000 per song
    - d. Lata Mangeshkar was getting Rs. 100–200 per song at the time
  - **Acting style**
    - a. known for North Indian Muslim feudal style acting ('adakari') in films including "Mirza Ghalib," "Phool," "Afsar," "Shayar," "Jeet" (1949), "Anmol Ghadi," and "Dastan"
    - b. this style endowed modernity with an aura of tradition, bringing a nostalgic feudal tinge to the scenes
    - c. her performance expertly integrated gestures, music, and speech
  - **Titles**
    Known in her heyday as 'Malika-e-Husn' (queen of beauty), 'Malika-e-Tarannum' (queen of melody), and 'Malika-e-Adakaari' (queen of acting).
  - **Songs**
    Across a career from 1936 to 1963, sang 338 songs in films (mostly her own films) plus 2 non-film songs, 'tum rahe kahan ko piya' and 'hum tum karenge pyar.'
  - **Filmography**
    - a. acted in 67 completed movies
    - b. excludes the incomplete film "Jaanwar" (with Dilip Kumar)
    - c. excludes the incomplete film "Paagal Khaana" (with Bharat Bhushan)
    - d. excludes an incomplete Indo-British film on Wajid Ali Shah (1953), directed by Herbert Marshall, with Ashok Kumar as Wajid Ali Shah
