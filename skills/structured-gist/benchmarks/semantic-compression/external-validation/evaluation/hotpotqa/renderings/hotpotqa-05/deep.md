- **Tralee Speedway**
  - **Type**
    A dirt track racing venue.
  - **Location**
    Jerrabomberra, on the New South Wales side of the ACT/NSW border, near Canberra, the capital city of Australia.
  - **Track**
    A 410 m dirt-surfaced oval.
  - **Past names**
    - a. Fraser Park Raceway
    - b. Pepsi Power Dome
  - **Legacy**
    Home to several drivers who went on to become Australian national and state champions.
- **Dirt track racing**
  - **Definition**
    Auto racing performed on clay or dirt surfaced oval tracks.
  - **Origin**
    Began in the United States before World War I.
  - **Growth**
    Became widespread during the 1920s and 1930s.
  - **Dominant car types**
    - a. open wheel racers, purpose-built racing vehicles, dominant in the Northeast and West
    - b. stock cars (fendered cars), dominant in the South, either purpose-built race cars or modified street vehicles
- **Australian Street Stock Championship**
  - **Type**
    A dirt track racing championship.
  - **Purpose**
    Determines the Australian national champion each year.
  - **Format**
    Held over a single meeting, usually on consecutive nights.
  - **History**
    Run annually since the 1989/90 season.
  - **Governing body**
    The Australian Saloon Car Federation (ASCF).
  - **Hosting**
    Awarded to a different Australian state each year.

- **Illinois State Fairgrounds Racetrack**
  - **Location**
    Illinois State Fairgrounds, Springfield, the state capital.
  - **Track**
    A one-mile-long clay oval, known as the "Springfield Mile."
  - **Construction history**
    Constructed in the late 19th century, reconstructed in 1927.
  - **Racing history**
    - a. hosted competitive auto racing since 1910, one of the oldest speedways in the US
    - b. oldest track to continually host national championship dirt track racing
    - c. first national championship race held in 1934, under the American Automobile Association banner
  - **Current events**
    - a. Allen Crowe Memorial 100 (ARCA stock car race)
    - b. USAC Silver Crown championship dirt cars
    - c. UMP Late Models and Modifieds
    - d. AMA Grand National Championship
  - **Records**
    Home to five world records for automobile racing, making it one of the fastest dirt tracks in the world.
