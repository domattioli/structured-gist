- **List of awards and nominations received by 2NE1**
  - **Group**
    A girl group formed by YG Entertainment in South Korea.
  - **Debut (2009)**
    First EP "2NE1" spawned hits "Fire" and "I Don't Care," one of the biggest singles of 2009, earning many awards and nominations.
  - **2010 album**
    First full-length album "To Anyone," with promotional singles "Clap Your Hands," "Go Away," and "Can't Nobody."
  - **2011 EP**
    Second EP "2NE1" earned many awards, including Album of the Year and Song of the Year for lead single "I Am the Best."
  - **2012–2013**
    - a. released four singles, including "I Love You" and "Missing You"
    - b. released their first full album in Japan
  - **2014 album**
    "Crush," with lead single "Come Back Home," entered the Billboard 200 chart.
  - **2017 farewell**
    Released the farewell song "Goodbye" in January 2017 after announcing disbandment.

- **Suraiya**
  - **Identity**
    Suraiya Jamaal Sheikh (15 June 1929 – 31 January 2004), an Indian Muslim Hindi/Hindustani film actress and playback singer in Bollywood from the 1940s to the early 1960s.
  - **Personal life**
    Remained unmarried after her love affair with actor Dev Anand was aborted by her maternal grandmother.
  - **Early career**
    - a. as a child singer until 1943, did playback singing for actress Mehtab in three films, and for her own child-artiste roles
    - b. acted as a heroine from 1946 to the end of her career in 1963
  - **Peak stardom (1948–1951)**
    Became Bollywood's singing superstar, earning more than any performing actor of the era, including Dev Anand, Raj Kapoor, Dilip Kumar, Nargis, and Madhubala.
  - **Earnings**
    Received 1.5–2 lakh rupees per film role, versus 50,000–1 lakh for top male actors, and commanded Rs. 1,000 per song versus Rs. 100–200 for Lata Mangeshkar.
  - **Training**
    - a. basic music training from a Hindustani teacher, arranged by her mother
    - b. first sang for a children's program on All India Radio in 1937
    - c. on-the-job vocal training from Naushad while recording
    - d. dance training from Mumtaz Ali, father of comedian Mehmood
  - **Style**
    Known for a North Indian Muslim feudal style of acting ('adakari') that lent modernity a nostalgic, traditional tinge.
  - **Titles**
    Called 'Malika-e-Husn' (queen of beauty), 'Malika-e-Tarannum' (queen of melody), and 'Malika-e-Adakaari' (queen of acting).
  - **Output**
    Sang 338 film songs plus 2 non-film songs, and acted in 67 completed films, across a career spanning 1936 to 1963.
