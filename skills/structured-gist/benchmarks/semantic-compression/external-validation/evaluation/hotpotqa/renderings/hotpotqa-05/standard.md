- **Tralee Speedway**
  - **Location**
    Jerrabomberra, on the New South Wales side of the ACT/NSW border, near Canberra.
  - **Track**
    A 410 m dirt-surfaced oval.
  - **Past names**
    - a. Fraser Park Raceway
    - b. Pepsi Power Dome
  - **Legacy**
    Produced several drivers who went on to become Australian national and state champions.
- **Dirt track racing**
  - **Definition**
    Auto racing performed on clay or dirt surfaced oval tracks.
  - **History**
    Began in the United States before World War I and spread widely in the 1920s and 1930s.
  - **Car types**
    - a. open wheel racers, dominant in the Northeast and West
    - b. stock (fendered) cars, dominant in the South, purpose-built or modified street vehicles
- **Australian Street Stock Championship**
  - **Purpose**
    Determines the Australian national dirt track racing champion each year.
  - **Format**
    Held over a single meeting, usually on consecutive nights.
  - **History**
    Run annually since the 1989/90 season.
  - **Governing body**
    Australian Saloon Car Federation (ASCF), which awards hosting to a different state each year.
- **Dirt track racing in the United Kingdom**
  - **Definition**
    Auto racing performed on oval tracks throughout the UK.
  - **Prevalence**
    Dirt ovals outnumber all other track types combined.
  - **Shared use**
    Tracks also host the motorcycle sport Speedway and other track racing events.

- **Dirt track racing in Australia**

- **Kings Park Speedway**
  - **Location**
    North-east of Regina and north-west of Pilot Butte, Saskatchewan, Canada.
  - **Operator**
    Regina Auto Racing Club.
  - **Track**
    A 1/3-mile high-banked paved oval, paper-clip style, with longer straights and tight turns.
  - **History**
    Originally a dirt oval, paved in 1970 and re-paved in 2007.
  - **Primary use**
    Stock car racing.
  - **2010 events**
    40th Anniversary "Kickin Asphalt" events, including Pro Trucks, Baby Grand racing, Legends of Alberta, Western Canada Super Late Model Racing, and Evolution Mini Cup Car series.
  - **Recent activity**
    - a. 2015: drifting, trailer racing, wheelies
    - b. 2016: regular Street Stock, Bomber, and Mini Stock class races
- **Illinois State Fairgrounds Racetrack**
  - **Location**
    Illinois State Fairgrounds, Springfield, the state capital.
  - **Track**
    A one-mile clay oval, known as the "Springfield Mile."
  - **History**
    Built in the late 19th century, reconstructed in 1927; hosting racing since 1910, one of the oldest US speedways and the oldest track continually hosting national championship dirt track racing since 1934.
  - **Current events**
    - a. Allen Crowe Memorial 100 (ARCA stock cars)
    - b. USAC Silver Crown championship dirt cars
    - c. UMP Late Models and Modifieds
    - d. AMA Grand National Championship
  - **Records**
    Holds five world records, making it one of the fastest dirt tracks in the world.
