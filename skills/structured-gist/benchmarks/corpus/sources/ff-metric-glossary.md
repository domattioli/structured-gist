Activation rate is the share of new signups that complete the setup wizard within seven days. Weekly active teams counts teams with at least three members active in a seven-day window. Time to first value measures minutes from signup to the first successful pipeline run. Churn rate is measured monthly on paying teams only, as cancellations divided by teams active at the start of the month. Net revenue retention compares this month's recurring revenue from teams that existed a year ago against their revenue a year ago. Support first-response time is measured in business hours, not clock hours. Error budget burn is the fraction of the monthly reliability allowance consumed so far. Deployment frequency counts production deploys per week across all services. Lead time for changes runs from first commit on a branch to production deploy of the merge. P95 query latency is sampled from the warehouse's read replica, not the primary. Seat expansion rate tracks additional licenses bought by existing teams per quarter. All dashboard metrics refresh hourly except revenue figures, which refresh daily after the billing batch.
