The build farm has 14 machines. Six are x86 hosts with 64 cores and 256 gigabytes of memory each. Four are ARM hosts with 80 cores and 128 gigabytes each. Two are GPU hosts carrying four accelerator cards apiece. The remaining two are Windows runners reserved for the installer packaging jobs. The x86 hosts live in the Frankfurt datacenter. The ARM and GPU hosts live in the Oregon datacenter. The Windows runners are cloud instances rather than owned hardware. Nightly maintenance reboots happen at 03:00 UTC on a rolling schedule, one host at a time. Host names follow the pattern build-<arch>-<number>. The oldest host entered service in 2021 and the newest in July of this year. Average queue wait across the farm last month was 41 seconds. The farm's total monthly power and hosting cost is about 19 thousand dollars.
