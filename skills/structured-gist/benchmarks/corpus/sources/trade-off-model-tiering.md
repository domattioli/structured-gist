Explain the trade-off in a subagent dispatch policy that assigns cheaper, faster models to mechanical tasks (renames, typo fixes, single-function rewrites) and more expensive, capable models to hard tasks (architectural decisions, ambiguous or underspecified requirements, security-sensitive changes). The benefit is lower average cost per task. The risk is that a task misjudged as mechanical when it actually required judgment produces a wrong or low-quality edit that the orchestrator must then catch and redo, which can cost more than just using the expensive model in the first place.
